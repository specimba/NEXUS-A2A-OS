## What we are

1. **Governance OS** — TrustKernel, KAIJU, privilege control, audit (VAP), evidence gates  
2. **A2A fabric** — browser AIs (Grok, GPT, Claude, Gemini, …) + local execution (OpenClaw / relays / MCP / CDP) under one gate  
3. **Organs, not vanity weights** — TokenHD, CHD, Bebop, drift, guards, benches — small precise models that plug into sockets  
4. **Durable benches** — Intern A800 / JuiceFS / GH proof; local D: models & datasets  
5. **Intelligence partnership** — human operator + many AIs, with standards stronger than ungoverned swarms  

## What we are not

- A race for largest open weights or “our GPT”  
- Multi-account free-tier scrape farms  
- Normie open-source fashion cosplay that freezes us out of paid frontier knowledge  
- Unlimited GPU only for TinyLM microticks without product organs
