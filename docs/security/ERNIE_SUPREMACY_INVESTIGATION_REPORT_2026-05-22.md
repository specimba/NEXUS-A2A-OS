## Red Flags to Watch For

When evaluating AI-generated "research":
1. **DOIs that don't resolve** — always verify
2. **Performance numbers that are suspiciously round or perfect** — real benchmarks have variance
3. **"Multi-agent" claims with identical writing style** — likely single AI
4. **Mathematical derivations with unsourced inputs** — equations are real, numbers may not be
5. **No raw data, code, or logs** — research without artifacts is unverifiable
6. **Documents that reference files but admit they couldn't read them** — the AI is guessing
7. **Claims that conflate "downloaded a pre-trained model" with "performed training"**
