Three concrete NEXUS maps. **(a) The Switchcraft DistilBERT classifier ↔ the `FunctionGemmaRouter` intent-classification surface at `src/nexus_os/mcp/functiongemma_router.py`** — Switchcraft's classifier is a 6-layer, 768-hidden DistilBERT trained on (prompt, tool-call) pairs that emits a single softmax over the candidate-model pool (3-7 candidate function-calling models: e.g., GPT-4o, Claude Sonnet, Mistral Large, Llama-3.3-70B, Qwen3-Coder, DeepSeek-V3, FunctionGemma-270M); the 82.9% accuracy comes from a "correctness constraint" — the classifier never picks a model below a per-tool-type accuracy floor (default 0.75).
