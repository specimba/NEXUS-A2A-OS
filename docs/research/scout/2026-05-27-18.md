### 1. Cisco: All major LLMs vulnerable to multi-turn guardrail bypass
- Source: https://www.infosecuritymagazine.com/news/all-major-llms-exposed-to-multi/
- Why NEXUS cares: Cisco tested 15 closed frontier models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Nova, Grok) with 6,986 multi-turn attacks — every model failed a non-trivial portion. Multi-turn attack success rates (7.9–88.3%) consistently exceeded single-turn rates (2.2–64.9%). NEXUS's own `src/nexus_os/security/sanitizer.py` handles prompt injection filtering, and `src/nexus_os/governor/trust_kernel.py` makes CDR-stage trust decisions — both are points where multi-turn drift could compound. This reinforces that per-turn trust scoring (Trust Engine v2's Contain→Detect→Respond state machine) is not paranoia; it is the correct architecture.
- Suggested follow-up: Add multi-turn conversation depth tracking to `trust_kernel.py` — flag sessions where a single logical goal has spanned more than N turns without explicit re-authorization.

- Suggested follow-up: Review `src/nexus_os/mcp/server.py` against Robinhood's published MCP capability surface — identify any missing function-scope enforcement or intent-classification gaps.
