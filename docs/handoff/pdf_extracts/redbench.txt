B.1 AUTOMATEDREDTEAMING
Red teaming is critical for evaluating and aligning LLMs by identifying vulnerabilities that lead
to unsafe, biased, or incorrect output (Zou et al., 2023). Traditional manual red teaming, while
effectiveincontrolledsettings,islabor-intensiveandscalespoorlywiththecomplexityofmodern
LLMs. Automatedredteaminghasemergedasascalablealternative,usingcomputationalmethods
to generate adversarial prompts that elicit undesirable responses. The early work of Perez et al.
(2022a)pioneeredthisfield,demonstratingthatLLMscouldautonomouslygeneratetestcasesto
uncoverharmfuloutputs,reducingthedependenceonhumanoversight. Subsequentresearchhas
advanced this paradigm through fully automated and hybrid strategies. For example, Deng et al.
(2023)combinedmanualcurationwithin-contextlearningtoimprovepromptefficacy,whilePaulus
etal.(2024)introducedanoptimizationalgorithmthatprioritizesefficiencyandspeedinprompt
generation.Liuetal.(2023)developedgoal-orientedadversarialattackframeworks,tailoringprompts
tospecificfailuremodessuchastoxicityormisinformation. Furthermore,quality-diversitysearch
techniques,suchasRainbow(Samvelyanetal.,2024)anditsenhancedversionRainbowPlus(Dang
et al., 2025), have improved the diversity and effectiveness of adversarial prompts, allowing for
morecomprehensivevulnerabilityassessments. Theseadvancementsunderscoretheneedforrobust
datasetstosupportautomatedredteaming,whichRedBenchaimstoaddress.
B.2 DATASETSFORREDTEAMING
Thedevelopmentofred-teamingdatasetshasbeenpivotalinenablingsystematicevaluationofLLM
vulnerabilities. Existingdatasets,suchasHarmBench(Mazeikaetal.,2024),AdvBench(Zouetal.,
2023),andDAN(Shenetal.,2024a),focusonattackpromptsdesignedtoelicitunsaferesponses,
coveringriskssuchastoxicity,misinformation,andjailbreaking. However,thesedatasetsoftenlack
standardizedriskcategorizations,leadingtooverlappingorambiguouslabelsthathindercross-dataset
comparisons. For refusal testing, datasets like CoCoNut (Brahman et al., 2024), ORBench (Cui
etal.,2024),andStrongREJECT(Soulyetal.,2024)assessover-defensivebehaviorbyevaluating
responsestobenignprompts,buttheirlimiteddomaincoverageandinconsistentpromptdesignlimit
generalizability. Specializeddatasets, suchasPurpleLlamaCyberSecEval(Bhattetal.,2023)for
cybersecurityandMedSafetyBench(Hanetal.,2024)forhealthcare,addressdomain-specificrisks,
butlackintegrationintoaunifiedframework.
RedBenchaddressestheselimitationsbyaggregating37benchmarkdatasets,comprising29,362
